# HKick Product Analysis

## Source: Nexte

Nexte positions itself as Morocco's football app with a simple promise: find games, book a spot, and play. The strongest ideas to keep are smart matchmaking, match/private chat, smart waitlist, level progression, club map, payments, and wallet. The weakness to exploit is that the public website still feels like a waitlist/marketplace story, not a live product experience.

## Source: FTBLR

FTBLR is closer to a football community: organize matches, join local games, find pitches, share videos, chat, invite friends, notifications, and multilingual support. The strongest ideas to keep are local community, match organization, pitch discovery, chat, notifications, and Arabic/French growth. The weakness to exploit is reliability and product focus: too much social content can dilute the core job of getting players onto a pitch quickly.

## Source: User Note

The handwritten framework is the right product method:

- Keep: what users already understand and need.
- Modify: what must become faster, clearer, and more premium.
- Remove: what creates friction, noise, or weak trust.

For HKick this becomes:

- Keep: matches, terrain booking, chat, profiles, skill level, community.
- Modify: make discovery real-time, team creation automatic, booking contextual, and availability visible.
- Remove: generic dashboards, empty states, noisy social feeds, unclear calls to action, slow booking paths.

## HKick Offer

HKick should not be "another football app." It should be the amateur football operating system:

- Uber-like: open app, see live supply, act immediately.
- Tinder-like: quick match fit, player availability, low-friction decisions.
- Airbnb-like: trusted terrain inventory, slots, pricing, booking confidence.

## UI/UX Direction Applied

- First screen now prioritizes live play intent.
- Empty database states now show polished demo content instead of looking broken.
- Match cards include fill speed, AI skill signal, waitlist signal, and terrain context.
- Terrain booking now looks like an operational marketplace, not a static list.
- The app includes a visible "Keep / Modify / Remove" strategic section to reflect the product critique method.
